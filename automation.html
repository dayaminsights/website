Nobody was hired to re-type things. Everybody does.

It never appears as a line on your P&L. It appears as evenings, mistakes and orders that slipped — and it gets worse exactly when business gets better. These are the four, and the four kinds of work in the next section are, one for one, what replaces them.

01

Your team is the integration layer.

Excel, WhatsApp, email, billing, a CRM someone set up two years ago. When systems don’t talk, a person carries the message — every time, all day.

02

Things get forgotten because somebody was busy.

The follow-up that never went, the reorder nobody raised, the quote that sat for a week. Not carelessness — just a process that depends on a person remembering.

03

The same document gets typed three times.

Once into the order sheet, once into the invoice, once into the ledger. Three chances to make a typo, and one customer who eventually finds it.

04

The same question, answered all day.

Is it in stock, what does it cost, when can you deliver. Your best people spend their afternoons on answers your own data already holds.
