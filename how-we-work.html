How we work

You built the business.
The rest is our job.

Every client reaches us having already done the hardest part — a business that works. What follows is the ladder we take it up, one rung at a time, with the tools each rung actually needs.

Yours Ours · five rungs

Handoff. That is where most businesses stop, and where we start. Everything after it is our work — you keep running the business while we take it up the next five rungs.

Rung 01Yours · done

Create

You built it. It already works.

A business that runs — on your judgement, your people and their memory. The processes exist; they just live in habits, sheets and heads rather than in systems.

What we doNothing. This rung is yours, and you finished it before you met us. It is also the only reason the rest is worth doing.

What changesYou arrive with something real to systematise, instead of a plan.

ToolsYour business, as it runs today
Rung 02Ours

Innovate

Rethink the process before automating it.

The obvious move is to automate exactly what you do today. Usually a third of it should not exist at all.

What we doWe redesign the process on paper first — cut steps, merge approvals, and agree what each number actually means.

What changesYou automate the process you should have, not the one you inherited.

ToolsNo software yet — your data, your team, a whiteboard
Rung 03Ours

Integrate

One number, entered once.

The same order lives in WhatsApp, a sheet and your billing software. People are the integration layer between them.

What we doWe connect the systems you already use, so data moves between them without a person carrying it.

What changesOne set of numbers that everybody trusts.

Automate

The repetitive steps stop being typed.

Your team’s day is made of steps a system could do: copying, chasing, sending, reconciling, reminding.

What we doOrder to invoice. Enquiry to follow-up. Report to inbox. The steps run themselves, with a person only where judgement is needed.
